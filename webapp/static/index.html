CHROMA Lab

live model — every panel is a real forward pass

1 · Tactile world

Eight sensor pads on one object. Click the canvas to move the agent; arrow keys also work.

2 · Predict → feel → error

The fast loop. Error in latent space is the one currency: it trains the predictor, weights the votes, and drives differentiation.

3 · Voting & posterior

Each pad forms its own hypothesis; they pool evidence through known relative poses. Bar = P(object).

4 · Genes — regulatory state g

The slow dial. Drag a gene and watch expression, plasticity and module identity change downstream.

5 · Expression, enhancers, differentiation

Rows are modules. Left: which gene programs each expresses. Middle: its sparse enhancer mask over g. Right: how plastic it still is.

6 · Attractor landscape

Basins of the regulatory dynamics, projected to 2D. Click a basin to jump the cell into it.

7 · Loop closure (Proposition 1)

Walk a closed path and return. Transport alone must land exactly where it began; anything left over is the learned residual.

8 · Mutation hypercube

The same architecture, group swapped to (Z/2)^L. Toggle mutations; additive effect is exact, the remainder is epistasis.
