Expression, enhancers, differentiation

Enhancer gates are continuous, not binary — the deeper the gold, the more of that regulatory dimension the module reads; an outline marks a gate above 0.5. On an untrained model most gates sit mid-range, so nearly every module reads a little of everything; L0 pressure separates them as it trains. Plasticity is η/η₀ = (1 − D)²: 1.00 is fully trainable, 0.00 terminally differentiated.
